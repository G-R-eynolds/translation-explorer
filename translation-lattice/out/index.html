A translation lattice for Belli’s Er giorno der giudizzio (1831)

Twelve line-for-line English translations, sampled under four stances, are clustered line by line by meaning rather than wording. Where the samples agree, the lattice narrows to one band. Where they disagree in meaning, it branches. Each coloured thread is one translation. The shaded band is the central “most likely” route. Click any node to see its variants and why the line branches.
